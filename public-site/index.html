A few things to know

How do I get an account?

Online accounts are not available on this public website yet. When resident services launch, accounts will be issued by the community office to approved residents.

Can everyone see resident records?

No. This website contains general information only and has no resident directory, financial records or private notices.

Who can enter a violation?

The resident portal is designed so only office administrators can enter or change community-rule violations. Residents will be able to read notices that apply to them. This feature is not available on this public website.

Does the portal collect payments?

No. This public website does not accept payments or collect card details.
